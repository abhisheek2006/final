Treatment and results

Is homoeopathy safe without side effects?

No treatment can be guaranteed to be free of side effects. Products labelled homeopathic may contain active ingredients and can cause side effects or interact with other medicines. Tell your qualified health care provider about any product you use, and do not delay or replace prescribed medical care.

Can I take homoeopathic medicines alongside allopathic medication, or should I stop my current medicines?

Homoeopathy is sometimes used as a complementary approach, but good-quality evidence has not established that it acts quickly for acute conditions or treats long-standing disease. Do not stop, reduce or change prescribed medicines; discuss any complementary product with your qualified health care provider.

What should I avoid while taking treatment?

The clinic advises avoiding Ayurvedic medicines, Ayurvedic toothpaste and products containing Ayurvedic ingredients while taking a remedy. Also avoid crude or raw herbal substances such as aloe juice, giloy juice or churan, and strong-smelling substances such as ashwagandha, camphor, menthol, coffee, dark chocolate and tamarind. Keep the medicine in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Do not stop prescribed medicines without speaking to the clinician who prescribed them.

Will homoeopathy work for my condition?

Ask us at your consultation and we will give you a direct answer. Some conditions respond well, some respond slowly, and a few need hospital care instead. We would rather tell you on the first day that homoeopathy is not the right tool for your problem than take your money and your time for six months.

For children and parents

Can homoeopathy be given to infants and children?

Newborns and children should be assessed by a qualified paediatric clinician before any medicine is given. If a homeopathic product is being considered, discuss it and the child’s other medicines with the clinician. Do not delay or replace recommended paediatric care, vaccines or prescribed medicines. No product can be promised to be free of adverse effects.

Is homoeopathy safe during pregnancy?

If you are pregnant and have a fever, flu-like symptoms, excessive vomiting or urinary symptoms, contact your obstetrician or doctor promptly. These symptoms may need timely assessment and treatment. Discuss any complementary product with your obstetrician first, and do not use it instead of prescribed care.

Can I give my child a remedy from an earlier consultation?

Please do not. A remedy is matched to one child’s symptom picture, and an old prescription may not be suitable for a new illness. Contact your child’s health care provider for advice about new or worsening symptoms.
